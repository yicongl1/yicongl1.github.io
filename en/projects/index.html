Research, development and outreach projects. The homepage shows a selection; this page lists everything.

Dunkirk urban heat island study (UTC-UR06)
Academic project · France

GIS-based study of the urban heat island effect in Dunkirk. Built a spatial methodology (land use, building energy consumption, green areas, water bodies, building thermal imaging), analysed interactions between building density, vegetation, water and urban temperature, and proposed planning improvements (greening, reflective paving, water protection, sustainable mobility). Personally responsible for data collection and processing, methodology design and the overall sensitivity analysis.

academic GIS spatial analysis
Bordeaux Airbnb price prediction (UTC-BA08)
Academic project · France

Prepared and cleaned a dataset of 11,000+ listings; exploratory analysis of correlations between variables; implemented and optimised linear regression, random forest, Ridge and Lasso models.
