Heat pump or adiabatic cooler? Building cooling across three climates (UTC-BA01)
Course project · France 2025

The same 6,204 m³ office building is placed in Compiègne, Marseille and Fort-de-France to compare air-to-air heat pumps, air-to-water heat pumps and a direct adiabatic cooler on energy, water, life-cycle impact and cost. The adiabatic unit uses about a third of the electricity, but fails above 75% humidity and has the highest water use.
