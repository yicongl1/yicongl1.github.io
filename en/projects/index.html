Dunkirk heatwave sensitivity (UTC-UR06)
Course project · France 2023

ArcGIS study decomposing building heat, vegetation cooling and water cooling into three continuous fields, combined with two-pass kernel density and multi-criteria weighting into a heatwave sensitivity index. The building effect is about 3.3× vegetation and 55× water.

academic GIS ArcGIS

Bordeaux Airbnb price prediction (UTC-BA08)
Course project · France 2024

11,854 Bordeaux listings from Inside Airbnb: EDA, maps and ANOVA, capacity features mined from titles, and a 100 € price split comparing linear regression, random forest, Ridge and Lasso. Random forest test R² 0.52 / 0.55.

academic data Python

Photovoltaic MPPT — P&O and its improved variant (UTC-BA04)
Course project · France 2024

Modelled a Kyocera KC200GT module in MATLAB/Simulink and compared P&O against the improved P&OA over 366 days of per-minute irradiance and temperature measured by UTC’s STELLA platform, in terms of annual energy and efficiency. Responsible for both algorithms, the year-long simulation and the result analysis.

academic MATLAB Simulink
