Urban Engineer · PhD applicant at Université de Technologie de Compiègne (UTC), class of 2026

I graduated in 2026 in Urban Engineering from Université de Technologie de Compiègne (UTC), with a B.Eng. in Information Engineering from Shanghai University. My research interests centre on access ergonomics and the socio-spatial inequalities linked to cross-border and electric mobility, with a view to supporting public decision-making through multi-scale analysis. I have worked on urban data analysis, GIS, database design, engineering simulation and construction-site coordination, as well as drones, football robots and an electronic-differential car — connecting spatial analysis, data engineering and embedded control. I work in Chinese, English and French.
